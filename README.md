# Compound-to-Ailment-Data-Model

### Role: Sole Developer / Data Architect

The Business Problem: In the highly regulated Ohio medical cannabis market, state law strictly prohibits retail staff from providing medical advice or product education. This regulatory bottleneck leaves patients guessing on product efficacy, leading to frustration and poor customer retention.

The Data Solution: I architected and developed a relational Power BI dashboard that serves as a self-guided educational tool. By matching specific chemical compounds (cannabinoids and terpenes) to documented ailments, the dashboard bypasses the operational bottleneck, allowing patients to make data-driven purchasing decisions safely and legally.

### Technical Execution:

ETL & Data Scraping: Extracted unstructured data from books and PDF research documents, converting scanned imagery into readable, structured datasets.

Data Modeling: Engineered complex, many-to-many relationships within Power BI to accurately link compound profiles with their corresponding medical applications.

UI/UX: Designed an interactive, accessible frontend interface tailored for non-technical users seeking immediate answers.

Future Scope: Future iterations would involve connecting the dashboard via API to live dispensary menus to overlay real-time inventory and pricing data onto the compound recommendations.
